Privacy policy

Chitchat is a small chat app run for a group of friends. This page explains what it stores and why.

What we store

  • Your account: email address, username, display name, and profile picture. If you sign in with Google or Discord, we receive your name, email and avatar from them, nothing else.
  • What you post: messages, reactions, and files or images you upload.
  • Settings: your voice and avatar choices. Some preferences (like volume) stay only in your own browser.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data, show ads, or track you across other sites.
  • Voice calls go directly between browsers (peer to peer) and are never recorded or stored.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage). The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Uploaded files are reachable by anyone who has their link.

Deleting your data

You can delete your own messages at any time. To delete your whole account and everything in it, ask the server owner or open an issue at github.com/DingoDevYT/chitchat.

Terms of use

  • Be decent. Don't post illegal content, harass people, or spam.
  • Server owners and admins can remove messages and members from their servers.
  • Chitchat is a hobby project provided as-is, with no guarantee of uptime or that data is never lost.
